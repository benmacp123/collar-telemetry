# What the long living still bouts are

Generated by `scripts/bouts.py` (`make bouts`); do not edit.

Stork tuning populations only. A bout is one of the mortality
detector's own clusters, run with no window, on a bird that was alive
(as in `results/stillness/STILLNESS.md`); only bouts longer than the 24 h
window are counted. Each is put in the first class whose test it
meets. The cut-offs were chosen by eye on these populations after
seeing the bouts: they describe, and no detector uses them.

| Class | Test |
|---|---|
| Nestling, before first leaving | Starts before the bird's first fix 500 m from where its tag started |
| Sparse fixes | Fewer than 6 fixes a day inside the bout |
| Back at the natal nest, first year | Within 500 m of where the tag started, under 365 days after its first fix |
| Site used in several years, breeding season | The bird has long bouts within 200 m in 2 or more calendar years; this one starts in months 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 at or north of 44 degrees |
| Site used in several years, other | The same repeat, at another season or further south |
| Site used in one year only | 3 or more long bouts within 200 m, all in one calendar year |
| One-off place | None of the above |

Counts are bouts, with the rate per animal-month in brackets. *Top
bird* is the largest share of the class's bouts that one bird holds.
*Spread* is the median hourly acceleration spread during the bout
(the acceleration cue's signal), with the bouts that had no reading
in brackets.
