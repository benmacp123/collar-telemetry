# How a still tag is judged to have moved

Generated by `make departure`; do not edit by hand. Each row makes one change to the box-only detector (100 m box, two fixes to leave, 48 h merge), except the radius rows. Death outcomes are *flapped / split / ended resolved*: deaths with a false "moving again" resolve taken back within the merge window, deaths raising more than one incident, and deaths whose last incident says the carcass moved. Outcomes and false-alert durations use evidence times, so they are shown under ideal arrival; detection by the mark is shown per scenario. Hours open are from opening to resolving, among resolved false alerts.
